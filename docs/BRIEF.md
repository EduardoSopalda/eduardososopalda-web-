# Brief

## The one sentence

Meaning is negotiated between people, and no tool, framework or model can do that
negotiation for you. Everything Eduardo writes is that argument in a different costume.

## Register

Pop surrealism, not solemn surrealism. The reference point is Mecano singing about Dalí in
1988: a serious subject delivered in the most accessible format available, with a joke in
the title. Warm, funny, slightly absurd, never reverent.

The interface is serious. The fragments are funny. That contrast is deliberate: the deadpan
environment makes the line land harder, and the calm reader is where the argument has to be
trusted. 404 and empty states are where humour costs nothing, so use them.

## Eduardo's voice, from a 21,586 word corpus

- Opens on a scene in present tense, no throat clearing
- Writes scenes and ratios, not aphorisms
- Distrusts industry statistics and dismantles them before offering a number the reader can
  measure themselves
- Credits people by name, relentlessly
- Domestic metaphors: a basement, a library burning, two librarians, a GPS with a 2003 map
- Dry humour buried mid sentence, never flagged
- Punchlines sit on their own line
- Ends on a real question addressed to the reader

## The three states

Field, then room, then reader. Noise, order, focus. The transitions are continuous, never a
page cut. The nerve runs through all three: horizontal and drifting in the field, tilting on
entry to a room, fully vertical as the reading spine.

## The seven stops

Manifesto, Writing, Lexicon, Out Loud, First Dates, Who, Contact.

Position on the line is an argument: state a position, show the work, then show the person.
LinkedIn is a small mark near the name, not a stop.

- **Manifesto** the argument stated once. Being written, do not draft it.
- **Writing** 25 signals, filterable by thread.
- **Lexicon** contested terms defined in public, with a note on who disagrees. Empty so far.
- **Out Loud** talks, panels, workshops, DAMA, a white paper in progress. Includes AUTOMA
  Chem 2026, Berlin, 26 October.
- **First Dates** the six part Speed Dating with AI series, its own room because it is a
  running format rather than a set of articles.
- **Who** the personal essays: Panama, Finland, Barcelona, a goat named Milly.
- **Contact** understated. No form. The question each piece ends on.

## Content model

One object type. A signal has a kind: article, note, talk, experiment, work, term.
Everything else is a relation.

- **threads** MEANING, GOVERNANCE, TRUST, PROXIMITY, MEMORY, CURIOSITY
- **people and orgs** pages that build themselves from what points at them. No curated list.
- **fragments** a line lifted from a signal, appearing in the field, linking back to the
  paragraph it came from.

Rooms are queries over this, not folders. Adding speaking is a kind plus three fields, not
a new template.

## The central test

A visitor can spend several minutes here without being asked to buy, book, subscribe or
contact anyone, and still leave with a clear sense of how Eduardo thinks.
